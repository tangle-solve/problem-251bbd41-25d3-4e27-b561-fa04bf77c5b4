### CHEM-000290: Direct asymmetric NH transfer to dialkyl sulfoxides
- **Subfield:** Round 2
- **Open:** Convert racemic dialkyl sulfoxides directly into enantioenriched unprotected NH-sulfoximines through catalytic NH transfer and simultaneous racemization.
- **Status:** Fan et al.’s 2025 iron/photoredox resolution performs well for many aryl–alkyl sulfoxides. The tested dialkyl substrate gave 29% yield and racemic product.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Provides compact sulfur stereocenters without chiral starting materials.
- **Key works:** F.-X. Fan, H. Xu, S.-X. Tang, Y. Dang and F. Wang (2025), *Iron-catalysed stereoselective NH transfer enables dynamic kinetic resolution of sulfoxides*, [DOI:10.1038/s41467-025-56860-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-56860-4).
- **Related:** Biophysics: selective recognition of nearly indistinguishable substituents.
